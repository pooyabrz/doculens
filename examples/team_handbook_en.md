# Example team handbook
This is a fictional document written for the DocuLens demo and tests.

## Annual leave
Full-time team members receive 24 days of paid annual leave each year. Submit leave requests to your direct manager at least three working days before the leave starts.

## Remote work
Team members may work remotely three days each week. Attendance at the Monday weekly meeting is required, either online or in person.

## Equipment
Equipment purchases require manager approval in advance. The annual equipment reimbursement limit is 300 USD. Submit the purchase receipt with the request.

## Collaboration hours
The team's shared collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 UTC. Members should be available for scheduled meetings during this period.

## Internal support
Record internal support requests in the request queue. The target time for an initial response is two working days.

## Learning
Each member may use four working hours per month for learning. The learning topic should relate to the member's role.
